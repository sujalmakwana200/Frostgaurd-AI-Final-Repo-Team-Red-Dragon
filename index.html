FrostGuard AI

Predictive cold-chain monitoring & rerouting for medical cargo

Vaccines, insulin, blood bags and organ-transport kits are ruined by a single unnoticed temperature excursion. Most fleet tools only alert after the limit is crossed. FrostGuard AI forecasts the breach first.

What it does

  • Forecasts each truck's cargo temperature 30 seconds ahead (HistGradientBoosting).
  • Flags abnormal thermal behaviour (Isolation Forest).
  • Recommends the nearest of 16 cold-storage facilities when a shipment is at risk (K-Nearest Neighbors).
  • Live map, fleet cards, voice alerts and a failure-injection demo mode.

Status

Working prototype built by Team Red Dragon at IIIT Vadodara. It runs on a simulated 13-truck fleet; models are trained on a synthetic thermal simulator and are not yet validated on real sensor data.
